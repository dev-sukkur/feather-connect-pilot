Who can sign in, and with what role. A viewer sees everything but PII, and changes nothing. An operator also runs, re-runs, backfills, retries, skips, replays, cancels, pauses and settles the quarantine. A developer also manages the environment's connections, budgets, secrets and alert rules and deploys packages, where the environment isn't protected. An admin can do all that anywhere, and manage users, their roles and API tokens too. Two grants go beside a role: an approver decides holds, and a PII reader sees masked fields as they are. A token acts with no higher a role than its user's, and with their grants, so a change shows in their tokens at once.
