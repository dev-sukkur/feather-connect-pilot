# Code of Conduct

Feather Connect adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place the
project meets.

In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and level of experience, give and take
criticism of the work rather than of the person, and don't harass anyone, in public or in private.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code to the maintainers privately, through the repository's Security tab
(**Report a vulnerability**, marked as a conduct report), or to a maintainer directly. Every report is reviewed and
answered, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential. The maintainers decide what to do, as the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe, from a warning to a permanent ban.
